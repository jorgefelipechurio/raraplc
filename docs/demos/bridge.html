Two separate connections that never touch. The board's serial port connects over direct USB to your device (Web Serial on desktop, or the Android app with WebUSB) — that never reaches the internet. Your device, separately, talks to Claude over your own data connection — that never enters the plant network. The board has no internet access, and never has.

WhatsApp is the conversation channel with Claude (the same thread you use for everything, support included). RARA Bridge is the tool you open here when the session needs a live view of the board CLI — what you see below. Claude never runs anything on the board: you run the commands, and Claude only reads what you share and suggests the next step.
